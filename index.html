Radia Research LLC

Early-stage antibacterial research and discovery

Focus

Radia Research develops early antibacterial discovery programs with an emphasis on rigorous prospective methods, chemical differentiation, structure-enabled discovery, and practical paths to experimental validation.

Current work

Current programs include structure-enabled discovery against Gram-negative antibacterial targets, with external medicinal chemistry and microbiology execution planned as programs advance.

Development approach

Radia uses public structural and chemical knowledge to build disciplined discovery workflows, preserve negative results and holdouts, and prioritize chemistry that can enter real biochemical and whole-cell testing cascades.
